PROJECT NOTE / PUBLIC CONTEXT

Ravenstar 是镜月工作室当前主要推进的两个项目之一，也是目前公开证据最完整的产品线。它以《泰拉瑞亚》模组为载体，把玩法系统、像素美术、叙事与长期内容制作放在同一条开发线上。

目前官网已收录两期 B 站阶段视频：2024 年的 E.G.O 武器完整展示，以及 2026 年的 E.G.O 系统重置展示。视频证明的是对应日期的开发状态；本站不据此判断是否已经存在其他测试、试玩或下载渠道。

在项目组补充正式试玩或下载地址前，本站只提供“观看进度”入口，不使用“立即试玩”一类可能造成误解的表述。

Ravenstar is one of the two projects Miroon Studio is pushing most actively, and it is currently the product line with the clearest public evidence. Built as a Terraria mod, it keeps gameplay systems, pixel art, narrative, and long-term content production on the same development track.

The website currently archives two public Bilibili progress videos: a full E.G.O weapon showcase from 2024 and an E.G.O system reset showcase from 2026. Those videos only verify the project's state on those dates; the site does not infer any other test build, playable demo, or download channel from them.

Until the team publishes an official playtest or download link, the site only presents a watch-the-progress entry point and avoids wording such as Play Now that could overstate availability.
