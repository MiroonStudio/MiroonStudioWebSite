MiroonShell is a terminal and permission-tool direction currently in planning, focused on identity verification, physical tokens, and local permission access. It belongs to the Studio Labs line.

At this stage, the public record only describes its research and design direction. The site does not present conceptual ideas as implemented features, and it does not make security claims in the absence of a threat model, implementation details, and audit evidence.
