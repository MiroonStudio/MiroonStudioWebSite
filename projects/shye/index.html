PROJECT NOTE / PUBLIC CONTEXT

公开的是研究定位，不是安全结论。What is public here is the research position, not a security conclusion.

  • 匿名通信Anonymous Communication
  • 网络协议Network Protocol
  • 无中心架构Decentralized Architecture
  • 前沿研究Frontier Research

Shye 是镜月工作室当前主要推进的研究项目，也是 Miroon Research 的核心协议方向。它面向通信关系不长期固定、参与者与网络条件持续变化的环境，探索严格无中心的通信架构如何被定义、验证与实现。

这里所说的“短期”，是通信关系与可观察结构不长期固定；“无中心”是严格无中心的设计目标，不代表当前已经实现或验证；“混沌”描述参与者与网络条件持续变化、难以预测的现实环境，不指数学混沌理论。

Shye 的理想终态是一项可验证的研究协议：具有严谨论文、参考原型与可复现实验，允许其他研究者提出质疑、复核结论并继续研究。这是研究目标，不是对当前完成度的陈述。

Research in progress · Manuscript in preparation。论文尚未公开，Studio 主站目前只解释 Shye 与镜月整体的关系。Shye 不是生产级协议或可部署服务，也不承诺绝对匿名、通信必达或已经证明的安全性。

Shye is one of the core research efforts currently being advanced by Miroon, and it is the central protocol direction under Miroon Research. It studies how a strictly decentralized communication architecture could be defined, validated, and implemented in environments where communication relationships, participants, and network conditions do not stay stable over time.

Here, short-term means that communication relationships and observable structures are not fixed for long periods. Decentralization is a design goal, not proof that it has already been achieved or validated. Chaos describes a real-world environment where participants and network conditions keep changing in hard-to-predict ways, not a reference to mathematical chaos theory.

The intended end state of Shye is a verifiable research protocol: a rigorous paper, reference prototypes, and reproducible experiments that allow other researchers to challenge assumptions, review the evidence, and continue the work. That is the research objective, not a claim about the current level of completion.

Research in progress. Manuscript in preparation. The paper is not public yet, so the Studio site only explains how Shye relates to Miroon as a whole. Shye is not a production-ready protocol or a deployable service, and it does not claim absolute anonymity, guaranteed delivery, or proven security.
