01 / STUDIO IDENTITY

寻那裂隙间，未熄的星火。Seek the ember in the rift that never went out.

镜月工作室制作游戏，也研究匿名通信与工程系统；从未知中寻找值得理解的可能，再把它们推进为可体验、可运行、可验证的作品。Miroon Studio builds games and studies anonymous communication and engineering systems, taking possibilities found in the unknown and pushing them toward work that can be experienced, run, and verified.

02 / MIRROR + MOON

名字来自镜与月，答案留在尚未说尽的故事里。The name comes from mirror and moon, and the answer lives inside a story not fully told yet.

Miroon 由 Mirror 与 Moon 组合而来。品牌更深层的叙事内核保存在独立游戏 StellarHush 的内部设定中，对外表达会避开剧情剧透。Miroon comes from Mirror and Moon. The deeper narrative core behind the brand lives inside the internal setting of StellarHush, so the public-facing explanation avoids story spoilers.

STUDIO NOTE / PUBLIC

Mirror 与 Moon，合成 Miroon。Mirror and Moon combine into Miroon.

对外，我们不急于解释所有答案。作品、研究与每一次公开进展，会逐渐构成这个名字真正的含义。Publicly, we are not in a hurry to explain every answer. The works, the research, and each visible update will gradually define what this name really means.

03 / ONE ORIGIN · TWO PRACTICES

一套共同的价值，两种不同的实践。One shared set of values, two different modes of practice.

Research 负责前沿议题的研究与论证；Studio 让研究和创意进入可体验、可运行的作品。Research takes on frontier questions and public reasoning, while Studio turns research and ideas into works people can experience and run.

05 / NON-COMMERCIAL BOUNDARY

共创可以开放，商业边界必须清晰。Collaboration can stay open, but the commercial boundary must stay clear.

镜月接受用于项目推进的自愿赞助，但目前不承接商业委托、不进行商业合作，也不开展商业活动。Miroon accepts voluntary support to help projects move forward, but does not currently take commercial commissions, partnerships, or any other commercial activity.
