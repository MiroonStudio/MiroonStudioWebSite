02 / FIELD NOTES

先看这些Start Here

01
敌方精神与精神崩坏Enemy Spirit & Spirit BreakEnemy Spirit & Spirit Break

精神伤害会同时压低敌怪独立的精神槽；清空后目标进入短暂崩坏，承受更高伤害并暂时失去碰撞攻击。Spirit damage depletes a separate enemy Spirit gauge; emptying it triggers a brief Spirit Break that increases incoming damage and disables contact damage.

→02
E.G.O 操作与统一按键E.G.O Controls & KeybindsE.G.O Controls & Keybinds

Ravenstar 将技能、换弹和同物品形态切换拆成三类固定操作，并在工具提示里显示玩家当前实际绑定的按键。Ravenstar separates E.G.O skills, reloading, and same-item form switching into three consistent inputs, with tooltips that show the player's current bindings.

→03
伤害属性与抗性Damage Types & ResistancesDamage Types & Resistances

Ravenstar 使用物理、精神、侵蚀与灵魂四种独立伤害属性；敌怪的抗性倍率决定每种属性最终造成的伤害。Ravenstar uses four independent damage types—Physical, Spirit, Erosion, and Soul—with enemy resistance multipliers determining final damage.

→04
小喙BeakBeak

能够在十二发手枪与大范围刀形态间切换的 TETH 级 E.G.O；受伤或使用刀形态命中会触发短时间惩戒强化。A TETH-grade E.G.O that switches between a twelve-round handgun and a wide-reaching blade; taking damage or landing blade hits triggers a brief Retribution boost.
