Prologue: Broken on Alien Soil

已公开序章记录了渡鸦从 L 公司废墟坠入泰拉，并在克苏鲁之眼之后因一场哥布林交火与玩家完成首次接触的经过。The published prologue follows Raven from the ruins of L Corp into Terraria, where a goblin firefight after the Eye of Cthulhu leads to first contact with the player.

**剧透边界：**本页只整理 v1.0.0 已经公开并接入游戏的序章，以及紧随其后的首次接触事件。这里不会展开资源危机、后续信任变化或任何尚未公开的章节。

两段序章演出

玩家第一次进入世界后，序章先展示从灰暗都市中升起的光之树。随后，R 公司要求第四集团军进入沉寂的 L 公司废墟回收样本与资料，并派出由维罗妮卡率领的渡鸦支队监督行动。

第一次击败克苏鲁之眼时，泰拉夜空中的猩红巨眼崩解。它死亡时掀起的异常波动穿过已经松动的世界边界，令 L 公司废墟中的回收行动偏离原有归途。

L 公司废墟中的撤离

第四集团军的兔子、驯鹿与犀牛进入封存区后，发现收容设施已经失效。没有抑制装置约束的第三阶段“微笑的尸山”冲向队伍；交叉火力与两次通道坍塌只能争取撤离时间，无法对核心生命信号造成有效损伤。

尼可莱最终下令终止回收。仍能移动的成员与渡鸦携带资料撤向 W 公司列车。尸山突破废墟并向车体喷出带有强腐蚀性的黑色尸块，车门则在倒计时结束时按预定程序关闭，把没能赶上的成员留在外面。

列车冲入空间裂缝后，克苏鲁之眼造成的跨界波动与失控跃迁发生交叠。列车和幸存者被抛出原有轨道，坠向不属于任何已知航线的泰拉大陆。

坠落后的静默

跃迁结束时，渡鸦无法解析坐标，与 R 公司的通讯全部中断。备用能源不足，克隆协议和渡鸦网络也处于离线状态。维罗妮卡命令清点幸存者与物资，并开始执行绝对独立行动预案。

他们同时检测到本地高能生命反应刚刚消失：击杀源只有一个人形个体，也就是玩家。渡鸦没有立即接触，而是记录目标、保持静默，并在腐化或猩红区域边缘建立地表临时基地。

枪声与基地外围

地表基地只会在克苏鲁之眼被击败后出现。序章演出结束后，玩家会收到来自世界某一方向的提示：一阵密集而规律的枪声正在远方响起。

玩家接近基地时，看见的不是一支即将被哥布林击溃的部队。渡鸦士兵与哨戒炮塔拥有足以持续清扫地表目标的火力；哥布林很难真正突破防线，却始终会从左右两翼得到补充。战斗因此陷入一种稳定而无法结束的消耗。

数秒后，陌生通讯确认了异常：

“地表目标数量没有下降。增援源位于防线下方；入口在两侧防线外缘。”

这时任务目标才从单纯观战转为“切断地下增援”。

玩家为何不可替代

渡鸦的优势是纪律、火力和固定阵地控制，但他们不能在维持地表防线的同时钻入地下、挖开地形。玩家熟悉泰拉的采掘与洞穴机动方式，因此承担双方第一次合作中唯一无法由渡鸦替代的工作。

防线两侧各生成一条永久地下通道。入口位于防线外缘，竖井向下延伸约 44 格，再通向基地下方的传送阵室。每个方向都有一个可由玩家挖掘破坏的承重节点；节点同时也是传送阵的控制核心。

  • 左侧节点存在时，左侧传送阵继续输送增援；
  • 右侧节点存在时，右侧传送阵继续输送增援；
  • 切断一侧后，该方向立即停止补兵，残余兵力会向仍开放的一侧集中；
  • 两处节点全部破坏后，地下增援彻底中断。

此后玩家回到地表，与渡鸦火力共同清理最后一批哥布林。这里体现的是职责互补，而不是渡鸦突然失去战斗能力。

第一次正式接触

最后一批哥布林倒在防线外后，残军迅速溃散，规律的枪声逐渐停止。守卫起初要求玩家停在原地并放下武器；维罗妮卡则撤销火力授权，要求先完成扫描。

扫描无法把玩家匹配到任何已知势力。维罗妮卡最终作出序章现阶段最重要的判断：

“记录为本地人形个体，暂定非敌对。”

她要求玩家前往基地入口进行说明。首次接触状态随之完成，入口门禁与守卫交互解锁。当前公开剧情到这里为止。

游戏内规则

  • 这是一场独立局部事件，不占用 Terraria 原版入侵槽位；
  • 两条坑道、节点位置与已经切断的路线都会写入世界存档；
  • 玩家暂时离开会清理当前事件敌怪，但已经挖出的通道和破坏进度不会消失；
  • 多人模式下，地形改动与事件阶段由服务器统一管理；
  • 本页不把设计文档中的后续章节当作 v1.0.0 已公开内容。

Spoiler boundary: This page covers only the prologue and first-contact event already published in v1.0.0. It does not discuss later resource crises, changes in trust, or any unreleased chapter.

Two Prologue Sequences

The first time a player enters a world, the opening shows the Tree of Light rising from the grey City. R Corp then orders the Fourth Pack into the silent ruins of L Corp to recover samples and records, with Veronica’s Raven detachment assigned to oversee the operation.

When the Eye of Cthulhu is defeated for the first time, the crimson eye comes apart beneath Terraria’s night sky. The wake of its death crosses a world boundary already coming loose and begins bending the recovery team’s route home.

Evacuation from the L Corp Ruins

Rabbits, Reindeer, and Rhinos enter the sealed sector and find that containment has failed. A third-stage Mountain of Smiling Bodies, no longer restrained by the facility’s suppression systems, charges the team. Crossfire and two deliberate corridor collapses buy time, but inflict no measurable damage on its core life signal.

Nikolai terminates the recovery operation. Everyone still able to move retreats toward the W Corp train while Raven personnel carry the recovered records. The Mountain breaks through the debris and vomits highly corrosive black corpse matter at the train, while the doors close on schedule and leave behind those who cannot reach them before the countdown expires.

The train enters the spatial rift. The crossworld wake produced by the Eye overlaps the failed jump, throwing the train and its survivors off the track and down toward a world belonging to no known route: Terraria.

Silence after the Fall

After the jump, Raven cannot resolve its coordinates and every link to R Corp is gone. Reserve power is low; both the cloning protocol and Raven network are offline. Veronica orders an immediate count of survivors and supplies, then activates the total-independence contingency.

The detachment also detects the disappearance of a local high-energy life-sign. The confirmed source is a single humanoid individual—the player. Raven records the target but maintains silence, establishing a temporary surface base near the boundary of the Corruption or Crimson instead of making immediate contact.

Gunfire at the Perimeter

The surface base appears only after the Eye of Cthulhu has been defeated. Once the post-Eye sequence has ended, the player receives a directional message: a dense and disciplined burst of gunfire carries from somewhere across the world.

Approaching the base does not reveal a force on the verge of defeat. Raven soldiers and sentry guns can continue destroying surface goblins with overwhelming firepower. The goblins cannot meaningfully break the line, but replacements keep arriving from both flanks, locking the battle into a stable and endless expenditure.

After several seconds, an unknown transmission identifies the actual problem:

“Surface target density is not falling. Reinforcement vectors originate below the line; access shafts lie beyond both flanks.”

Only then does the encounter reveal its objective: cut off the underground reinforcements.

Why the Player Is Necessary

Raven controls fixed ground through discipline and firepower, but cannot abandon that line to excavate beneath it. The player understands Terraria’s mining and cave movement, and therefore performs the one task Raven cannot replace during this first cooperation.

One permanent tunnel is generated outside each defensive flank. A vertical shaft descends roughly 44 tiles before turning toward a portal chamber beneath the base. Each route ends at a mineable load-bearing brace that also serves as the portal’s control core.

  • while the western brace remains, the western portal continues supplying troops;
  • while the eastern brace remains, the eastern portal continues supplying troops;
  • sealing one route stops its reinforcements immediately and pushes remaining forces toward the open side;
  • destroying both braces cuts off the underground supply completely.

The player then returns to the surface and helps Raven clear the final goblins. The encounter is about complementary roles, not a sudden loss of Raven’s combat ability.

Formal First Contact

Once the last wave falls outside the defensive line, the survivors scatter and the disciplined gunfire fades. The guards initially order the player to remain still and lower their weapon; Veronica revokes fire authorization and requests a scan first.

The scan finds no match among known faction signatures. Veronica makes the decisive judgement that ends the current public prologue:

“Log the subject as a local humanoid. Provisional status: non-hostile.”

She orders the player to proceed to the entrance so terms can be established. First contact is marked complete, unlocking the base entrance and guard interaction. The currently published story ends here.

In-Game Rules

  • the firefight is a local event and does not occupy Terraria’s vanilla invasion slot;
  • tunnel geometry, brace positions, and sealed routes are saved with the world;
  • leaving temporarily clears active event enemies, but does not erase excavated tunnels or completed objectives;
  • in multiplayer, the server owns terrain changes and encounter progression;
  • this page does not present later design-document chapters as content published in v1.0.0.
