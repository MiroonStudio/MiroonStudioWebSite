Wasp

技能说明Skills

主动：Active: 「长蜂」“Long Wasp”

  • 中远距离精准射击，命中会施加蜂针标记
  • Delivers precise mid- to long-range fire; hits apply a Needle Mark

主动：Active: 「短蜂」“Short Wasp”

  • 近距离蜂群散射，对已标记目标造成更高伤害
  • 按右键切换模式，按换弹键重新装填
  • Fires a close-range swarm spread that deals more damage to marked targets
  • Right Click switches modes; press Reload Key to reload
「WAW 级 E.G.O」「WAW-class E.G.O」
